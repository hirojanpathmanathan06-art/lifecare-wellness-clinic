Caring for you and your family

LifeCare Wellness Clinic is a private medical clinic in Colombo offering general check-ups, specialist consultations and wellness programmes. Our online system lets you register, book appointments and get care without long queues or paper forms.

Book an Appointment Register as a Patient

Our Services

Our doctors and nurses look after patients of every age, from routine check-ups to specialist care.

General Check-ups

Routine health checks, blood pressure and blood sugar tests, and treatment for common illnesses with our general physician.

Specialist Consultations

Appointments with our cardiologist, paediatrician, dermatologist and orthopaedic surgeon on set days each week.

Wellness Programmes

Full health check packages with laboratory tests, ECG and advice on diet, exercise and healthy living.
